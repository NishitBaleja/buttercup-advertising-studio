Why are We Fit for your business?
Whether you are in the B2B, B2C or D2C segment, or you are a price, masstige, premium, or a luxury brand, we start with the strategy focused on your target audience.

Why Hire us?
We don't always put ourselves in your shoes! Rather, we strategize taking into consideration the preferences of your customers.

We don't think "out of the box" because there is no box for us. You will have the advantage of choosing from diverse perspectives and choosing the most optimized strategy.

We have qualified, skilled stalwarts as well as young professionals in-house who bring fresh ideas and the latest trends to the table.

We provide Complete Creative Branding Services Solutions in one place.

How many Projects have we worked on?
Having worked for multiple product/service categories across diverse industries in the last 6 years we have developed the competencies to guide our clients towards the right branding direction.

We have successfully completed more than 400 projects for our clients.

( Message from the Creative Director & Founder )
Sneha Vakta
Creative Director & Founder

Creative Director & Founder

I heartily welcome you to Buttercup Advertising Studio. We are proud to be based in the state of Gujarat, India which has gifted some iconic brands to the world.


All over the business world, Corporates, SMEs, and start-ups are trying to gain market share by creating powerful brands.


Often they are time-starved to understand the process of brand creation. Sometimes they confuse branding with design or advertising.


I hear a lot of experiences from my clients that they have paid huge amounts of money to different agencies providing specialist services and still they were not happy with the deliverables.

( 3 problems we solve )

This inspires me to solve
the 3 problems of clients:

01The Challenge

Providing a one-stop destination for all branding requirements namely strategy, design, marketing communication and digital marketing

Our Solution

Our clients save their time and money in searching and coordinating with different agencies; instead, we deliver all branding and marketing services in one place under a unified, disciplined strategy.

02The Challenge

Getting the brand positioning right and helping clients avoid costly mistakes

Our Solution

A lot of business owners and their teams do not have clarity about positioning aspects and techniques of differentiation in crowded markets. They often jump directly into advertising without desired results. We help our clients follow a disciplined, logical approach where advertising comes only after the brand identity is properly created.

03The Challenge

Conceptualizing, designing and running marketing campaigns for maximum return on investment (ROI) with transparency

Our Solution

Often, business heads have a hard time deciding the best platforms for advertising and allocating their marketing budget. We help our clients understand the nuances of marketing communication including digital marketing and social media management. We run all types of online, offline, and integrated marketing campaigns with clear business objectives.
