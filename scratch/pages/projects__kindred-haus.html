Kindred Haus

Services

Branding / Website

Overview

Kindred Haus wanted a serene online home that mirrored their passion for well-crafted, story-rich kitchenware from around the world. We created a clean, minimalist brand identity with soft neutrals, elegant typography, and subtle playful accents, then built a smooth Shopify site with curated collections, product storytelling, and easy navigation. Free shipping thresholds and a gentle vibe encourage visitors to slow down and savor the little joys—perfectly inviting and oddly delightful.
